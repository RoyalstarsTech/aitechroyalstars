Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a laptop?

No. A smartphone is enough. Everything you build in class — WhatsApp Business, Google Business Profile and your content — is done on your phone.

Where does the class hold?

Online, live on Zoom or Google Meet. Materials, recordings and the 30-Day Challenge run in our class Telegram group. You'll get the joining link in the WhatsApp group.

How much data will I need?

Plan for about 1.5GB per live session for a stable video call.

Do I need to pay for AI tools?

No. We use only free versions of tools such as ChatGPT, Google Gemini, Meta AI, Microsoft Copilot, Canva and CapCut. No dollar card needed.

How much is the training and when is the next class?

The fee and class dates are shared in our WhatsApp group. Register and join the group to receive them first — early registrants also get the early-bird bonus.

What if I miss part of a session?

Recordings and all materials are shared in the class Telegram group, so you can catch up.

Will I get a certificate?

Yes. Everyone who attends the full class gets a Certificate of Participation. Participants who complete the 30-Day Patronage Challenge can earn the Certified AI Customer Growth Practitioner certificate. All certificates can be verified on this website.

I'm not a business owner yet. Can I join?

Yes. Undergraduates, school leavers and aspiring entrepreneurs are welcome. You'll practise with a business idea or a product you'd like to sell.
