Questions

Frequently asked questions

I'm not good with phones or English. Can I join?

Yes. You can talk to AI with your voice — no typing or big grammar needed. Every exercise has an Easy Lane, and our team will help you step by step.

I don't have a business yet. Is this for me?

Yes. The Start-Up Lane is for students, graduates, NYSC members and anyone starting out: find an idea, test it cheaply, write a simple plan and get your first customers.

I already use WhatsApp and ChatGPT. Will I learn anything new?

Yes. The Sharp Lane goes further: money calendars, price talk, record keeping, quotations and proposals, customer follow-up systems and showing up when people ask AI.

Do I need a laptop?

No. A smartphone is enough. A laptop helps for the Sharp Lane record sheets, but it's optional.

Where does the class hold?

Online, live on Zoom or Google Meet. Materials, recordings and the 30-Day Challenge run in our class Telegram group. You'll get the joining link in the WhatsApp group.

Do I need to pay for AI tools?

No. Everything works with free versions of Meta AI, ChatGPT and Google Gemini. No dollar card needed.

How much is the training and when is the next class?

The fee and class dates are shared in our WhatsApp group. Register and join the group to receive them first — early registrants also get the early-bird bonus.

Will I get a certificate?

Yes. Everyone who attends both sessions gets a Certificate of Participation. Participants who complete the 30-Day Smart Sales Challenge can earn the Certified AI Customer Growth Practitioner certificate. All certificates can be verified on this website.
